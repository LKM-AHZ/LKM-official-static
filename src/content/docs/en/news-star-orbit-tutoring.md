Star Orbit Tutoring Studio is an all-subject tutoring studio under Phientist, currently in the planning and preparation stage.

The studio serves students across multiple grades and subjects, providing customized tutoring based on each student's current level and goals. Through careful assessment and planning, we want learning support to fit each person's pace of growth.

## What we do

- Cover multiple grade levels and subjects;
- Build personalized growth plans based on ability and goals;
- Match students with suitable teachers and online tutoring;
- Support communication and matching for families, students, and channel partners.

## Teachers and partners welcome

We welcome capable teachers, as well as students and families who are looking for support.

If you have a channel — a school, a community, or another network — and would like to help launch online tutoring services, please get in touch. The studio will handle organization and matching so the right people can find the right needs.

## Working with the Public Benefit Program

Star Orbit Tutoring Studio is planned to launch alongside the Public Benefit Program.

The Public Benefit Program serves broader learning needs. Where there is a higher level of need and a personalized growth plan is required, students can turn to the studio for support.

## Revenue sharing and transparency

The current plan is a 50/50 split between the individual and the studio.

Once the studio is stable, with more channels, more tutors taking orders, and reliable revenue, the individual share will rise to 60% or 70%.

All studio expenses and income will be made public. The organization's share will be invested entirely in building the organization.
