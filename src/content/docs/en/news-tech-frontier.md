## What “Frontier” Means Here

Frontier is a noun and a stance. The Science Frontier column only picks things worth reading further: research worth explaining carefully, tech shifts with public value, and science anyone can discuss. We’d rather post fewer, better pieces than chase a daily feed.

Unlike a news ticker, each post gives context — why it matters, what researchers are solving, and what it implies for the rest of us. If parts are hard to follow, that’s expected; explaining is basically the point.

## How We Pick & Improve

Topics first pass our editor notes, then a review pass checks the writing is clear and the claims keep their limits — never selling “maybe” as “certain.”

The column updates on a loose schedule, and we hope it isn’t one-way. If you see something other LiKeMi members should read, forward it. A single link is enough — leave the unpacking to us.
