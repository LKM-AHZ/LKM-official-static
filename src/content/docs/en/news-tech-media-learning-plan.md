The Tech Media Learning & Exchange Plan is currently underway and recruiting.

We plan to have people with media experience act as teachers for those who want to learn the craft. Through this program, we also hope to bring more people into the Phientist July Team News Office to take charge of promotion and operations.

## What we do

- Pair experienced creators with learners and help them move from content planning to publishing and operations;
- Train News Office members in promotion, operations, and content creation;
- Invite science and learning creators from different platforms to join the conversation, learn from one another, and build connections.

## Who it is for

- People who want to learn media skills but do not know where to start;
- Experienced creators who want to exchange ideas with peers and look for collaboration;
- Anyone interested in promotion, operations, or content creation who wants to help Phientist reach a wider audience.

We continue to hold to openness, sharing, cooperation, and inclusiveness. Whether you are just starting out or already creating steadily on your own platform, you are welcome to join.
