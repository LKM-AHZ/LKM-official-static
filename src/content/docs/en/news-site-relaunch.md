Last summer, less than two or three months after our new website went live, we suffered an accidental database deletion. The content we had built up could not be recovered, and that loss is something we still regret.

This time, we are back with a completely new official website. Its framework was built by Phientist's own Technology Committee, and the site is now deployed independently. We hope it becomes a clearer, more stable window into who we are — and a place where more science and technology enthusiasts can find us and join us.

## 1. What has changed?

Previously, the website served two roles at once: a public-facing official site and an internal knowledge platform shared by the whole community — a place where anyone could edit and upload, with presentation and promotion treated as secondary.

The new site is deployed independently and has a clearer purpose: introducing the team and showing what the team is working on. The collaborative knowledge platform will continue on a new website.

### 1.1 A fuller picture of the team and our philosophy

The home page brings together Phientist's history since 2014, changes to the team, the people behind it, and the ideas and work we are pursuing.

If you want to learn about a particular team system or department, or start a discussion about an idea, you can find the right people in the [Team](/en/team) section. Every department has an entry explaining what it does and roughly what kinds of skills it needs, so you can get oriented quickly.

### 1.2 Faster, more accurate access to information

The [News](/en/news) item in the navigation brings together all current content sections and will grow with new team systems in the future.

Whichever category you click, the tag bar below switches with it. Announcements, operations notes, and update information are collected here, so you can follow the area you care about and filter for what you need.

The "Website & Community" tab is dedicated to website operations and update progress, making those posts easier to find.

### 1.3 Join the team you are interested in

The [Projects](/en/products) item introduces the projects we are running and planning, the studios behind them, and the products we have produced.

Each card shows its current status:

- Products: whether they are live;
- Projects: whether they are in preparation, recruiting, in progress, or have produced results;
- Studios: whether they are currently open or paused.

The cards also describe the work, the project's goals, the services a studio offers, and what a product does, so you can judge whether it is a good fit.

If a team or studio catches your interest, or you think a product could be improved, you can reach us through the contact details at the bottom of every page, or email us at [almafox123@outlook.com](mailto:almafox123@outlook.com).

### 1.4 A larger community

The [Community](/en/about#communities) page brings together Phientist's Tencent QQ groups.

Compared with last year, we have expanded beyond applied science and added an applied-subjects community system, serving more enthusiasts' needs for specialist discussion, shared interests, and recurring activities.

In the sidebar you can choose the age group and community category that fit you, including students in international curricula, students studying abroad, and international friends. More categories will be added as the team and community grow.

### 1.5 Extended resources

Beyond our own content, we provide a separate [Resources](/en/resources) page maintained by a dedicated volunteer, who regularly collects and publishes links to useful external sites.

If you have a resource to recommend or would like to help maintain the page, you are welcome to join us through the existing contact channels.

## 2. Where did the missing part of the site go?

The official website now has a clearer purpose: introducing the team and the projects the team is working on.

The part of the site that was built and shared by the community, along with the functions it supported, will continue on a new website. That site will place a stronger emphasis on learning. It is currently in preparation — stay tuned.

## A final note

That is an overview of the new website. To explore further, join us in the [Phientist QQ community](/en/about#communities).

Launching is only the starting point. We will continue to add community introductions, usage guides, and maintenance documentation, and turn records of events and projects into articles that can be referenced over the long term. If you are new, start with [Getting Started](/en/docs/getting-started); if you would like to help maintain content, see the [Editing Guide](/en/docs/editing-guide).

If you find a broken old link, a layout issue on a particular device, or an explanation that is not clear enough, please send the page URL and a screenshot to the maintainers. We will turn every reproducible piece of feedback into the basis for the next small improvement.

Thank you for supporting Phientist, and for caring about the work we do and the ideas we stand for.

We will keep working to bring science and technology enthusiasts a better experience.
