To make our existing groups genuinely active, and to help more members grow from participants into builders, Phientist is officially launching the Group Adoption Program.

This is a long-term program. Phase one covers existing groups and interest sections; phase two supports branches and new groups. We want to hand authority to people who are willing to take responsibility, and hand groups to people who truly want to make them work.

## Program at a glance

- **Phase 1**: adopt an existing group or interest section, form a team of 2–3, and complete a 3–4 month assessment.
- **Phase 2**: start a branch or a new group, form a team of 2–4, and complete a six-month assessment.
- **Long-term**: outstanding participants will be invited to join the organization and may take on more responsibilities.

## 1. About the program

This program responds to the current state of our groups. It is designed to strengthen group building, expand the organization, and energize the community, and it puts the idea of "Phientist builds Phientist" into practice.

Phase one covers existing groups and interest sections; phase two will support more high-quality discussion groups and clubs. The program is long-term.

### How to adopt

1. Choose one or two groups you are strongest in and most willing to build.
2. Write a short group-building plan.
3. Form a team of 2–3; members may include current Community Affairs Group or Event Planning Department members.
4. Tell Qianxun your intention, your plan, and your team members.

The team is responsible for the group's atmosphere and event planning.

### Assessment and support

The adoption period is 3–4 months. If the group becomes more active and satisfaction with events and operations reaches two-thirds or higher, team members will be invited to join the organization as formal members.

The Event Planning Department will provide support and training for phase-one participants, using case studies and hands-on practice to help people grow. The pass rate will not be low.

If satisfaction is below two-thirds and activity or retention remains low, the adoption is considered unsuccessful; some permissions will be withdrawn, and outstanding individuals will still be invited.

If the group reaches the point where it must be dissolved or its permissions revoked, the initiator should explain the reasons to the organization and may request a 1–2 month extension, or a new initiator may take over.

## 3. Phase two: start a branch or a new group

Existing groups may open a branch, or members may start a new group. Examples include materials science or psychology groups under the chemistry group, a new engineering general group (computers, chips, electronics), or an earth science general group.

### What to prepare

- A group-building plan;
- A founding team of 2–4, which may include current organization members;
- The initiator as the core lead;
- A team that ideally includes community affairs, general affairs, event planning, and professional members (professional members are not mandatory).

### How it starts

Content discussion groups start as a "Discussion Group"; interest sections start as a "Club". General Affairs creates the group and delegates authority, followed by a six-month assessment.

The organization will provide training and advice. If you dare to think, act, and take responsibility, the organization will support you fully.

### Assessment and upgrade

If the group reaches 300 members, runs normally, and member satisfaction with the team reaches two-thirds or higher:

- A "Club" becomes an interest section group;
- A "Discussion Group" becomes a subgroup or general group;
- All members are invited into the organization and rewarded.

If satisfaction is below two-thirds and management is chaotic or negligent, the group will be publicly dissolved or its permissions revoked, and it will return to phase one for adoption. Outstanding individuals will still be invited.

If the group reaches the point of dissolution or revocation, the initiator may explain the reasons and request a 1–2 month extension, or a new initiator may take over.

## 4. For current organization members

Organization members need to move and grow, but there should be no redundancy. Members who actively take part in group building will be rewarded; those who are willing and capable are welcome to take on more roles in General Affairs.
