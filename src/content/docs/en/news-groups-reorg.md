## Why Split the Channel

Our members range from middle school to senior year, and their stages rarely line up on the same
topics. Middle school members are more often working through foundations, study habits, and a
first taste of competitions. High school members are more often discussing subject choices,
competition schedules, and tighter academic plans. Rather than squeezing everyone into one room
to compromise, we split into spaces that fit better — that was the point of this reorg.

So there are two parallel official groups: Phientist · Middle School and Phientist · High School.
The middle school group focuses on foundations and guided learning; the high school group focuses
on competitions, subject choices, and more advanced study. Threads become clearer, and the people
answering are more likely to share your stage.

## What Each Group Is For

- **Middle school**: making "where do I start" clear. Basic concepts, study methods, exploring
  interests, and preparing for a first event are all fair questions.
- **High school**: making "how do I keep a steady pace" clear. Competitions and extensions,
  subject selection, time management, projects, and further study fit better here.
- **Shared ground**: community announcements, event information, cross-stage science content,
  and long-running projects remain open to everyone. Splitting the groups does not cut the
  community in half.

The split is meant to bring daily discussion closer to people's actual questions. Joint events,
talks, and project recruitment are still announced across groups.

## Joining and Verification

Each group has a short introduction and a verification step. Check which one fits you before
joining. Verification is not a barrier; it keeps advertising, impersonation, and unrelated
content down so daily conversation stays stable.

If you are unsure, choose by your current stage. You can ask to move if your stage changes.
Parents, teachers, and members who have already graduated can choose by the topics they mainly
care about.

## Light Group Rules

The split also clarified the direction for posting and asking questions; each group's
announcements take precedence in practice:

- Search the announcements and pinned messages before asking, to avoid repeats.
- When asking about a problem, share your own reasoning instead of only posting the question and
  waiting for an answer.
- Do not spread unverified claims or repost group screenshots publicly.
- Discuss privacy, account, or security matters with an administrator privately.
- Questions from any level are welcome; mocking basic questions will be addressed.

## After the Split

The next step is making joining obvious: each route has a clear intro and verification, so
choosing a group isn't guesswork, and light posting rules keep the daily feed useful.

If you or a friend are in that stage and want back in, pass this note along. We split to grow
together, not apart.
