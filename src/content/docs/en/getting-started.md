The LiKeMi static website is designed primarily for reading and requires no account.
Its pages are generated from Markdown, making them easy to bookmark, share, and archive.

## Browse the Site

1. Start from the home page or the news list.
2. Use the language navigation to switch between Chinese and English content.
3. Share an article by copying its URL; published paths should remain stable.
4. Dates, registration instructions, and external links may change. For active events,
   prefer the newest announcement on the same topic.

## Report a Problem

Include the page URL, the affected text or a screenshot, and the correction you propose.
Changes to historical claims require a source or maintainer confirmation; layout, translation,
and broken-link fixes can be submitted directly.

## Privacy

Static pages do not ask for account credentials. Once you follow a link to another service,
that service's privacy policy and terms apply. Never post passwords, verification codes,
access tokens, or other secrets in a public report.
