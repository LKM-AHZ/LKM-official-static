The Phientist static website is designed primarily for reading and requires no account. Its
pages are generated from Markdown, making them easy to bookmark, share, and archive.

## Choose a Starting Point

- New to the community: start with [Welcome to Phientist](/en/docs/intro).
- Looking for recent updates: open the [article list](/en/news) and browse by date or tag.
- Searching for a topic: use the search box above the list, or select a tag.
- Interested in maintenance: read the [Editing Guide](/en/docs/editing-guide).

## Browse the Site

1. Start from the home page or the news list.
2. Use the language navigation to switch between Chinese and English content.
3. Share an article by copying its URL; published paths should remain stable.
4. Dates, registration instructions, and external links may change. For active events, prefer
   the newest announcement on the same topic.

## Search, Tags, and Article Pages

The article list searches titles, summaries, and tags. The tag bar shows the tags currently in
use and how many articles each contains. Search and tags can be combined to narrow the list
quickly.

On article pages, the table of contents in the right column lists headings from levels two to
four and highlights the section you are reading as you scroll. Long lists scroll independently;
on narrower screens the table of contents is hidden and the article simply flows in order.

The top of an article shows its publication date and an estimated reading time. At the end,
previous/next links let you continue in chronological order. The theme button in the header
switches between day and night modes and remembers your choice in the browser.

## Sharing and Offline Copies

Copy the address bar to share a single article. Because the whole site is static, the browser's
print-to-PDF or reader mode usually preserves the layout. For long-term archiving, save the title
and publication date as well, so future articles with similar names do not get confused.

External links can expire or lead to third-party pages. When archiving, record the original
source rather than relying only on a screenshot.

## Report a Problem

Include the page URL, the affected text or a screenshot, and the correction you propose. Changes
to historical claims require a source or maintainer confirmation; layout, translation, and
broken-link fixes can be submitted directly.

For security issues, member privacy, or account matters, contact maintainers privately instead
of posting publicly.

## Privacy

Static pages do not ask for account credentials. Once you follow a link to another service,
that service's privacy policy and terms apply. Never post passwords, verification codes, access
tokens, or other secrets in a public report.
