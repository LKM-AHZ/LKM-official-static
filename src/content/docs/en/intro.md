Phientist is a learning community for people interested in science and technology. It began in
2014 as a small chat group and has grown into a place for discussions in mathematics, physics,
chemistry, biology, computing, and related fields — as well as a record of community events,
projects, and long-running work.

We care more about whether we can figure a problem out together than about who sounds more
professional. Whether you are meeting a subject for the first time or already preparing for
competitions and building projects, you should find people here who are willing to engage
seriously.

## What This Site Contains

- **Announcements and news** about community changes, activities, and projects.
- **Durable reading material** stored as Markdown and published as static pages, easy to search,
  archive, and maintain.
- **Two language entrances** under `/zh/` and `/en/`. Translations aim for the same meaning and
  context rather than word-for-word equivalence.
- **A community record**: team structure, timeline, external resources, and group entry points
  in stable pages.

This static site does not handle accounts, posting, or real-time interaction. Those features
belong to the PHIENTIST community service; this site focuses on reliable public information.

## How the Site Is Organized

- **Home**: who we are, recent updates, timeline, and common questions.
- **Articles**: announcements, news, guides, and introductions. Article pages include a table of
  contents, reading time, and previous/next links.
- **Team**: the July Team, its departments, members, and ways of working.
- **Resources**: academic institutions, research tools, and literature sites.
- **Community**: official groups, subject boards, and how to join.

## Where to Begin

1. Read [Getting Started](/en/docs/getting-started) for language switching, search, and feedback.
2. Browse the [article list](/en/news) or the home page's recent updates to see what has changed.
3. To meet people, start with the [community page](/en/communities).
4. To help maintain content, read the [Editing Guide](/en/docs/editing-guide).

## Taking Part

The community is maintained by its members. You can join an official group, volunteer for an
event, contribute a science or project article, or simply read — if the site is useful to you,
that already counts.

If you find an error, a broken link, or an awkward translation, tell us. Including the page URL
and the exact location helps us fix it faster.

## A Note

Phientist is not a school and does not replace systematic study. It is a place to ask questions,
try ideas, and keep talking over time. Please respect different backgrounds and viewpoints,
protect your own privacy and others', and keep disagreement about ideas rather than people.
