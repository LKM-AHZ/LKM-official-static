## A Steadier New Start

This relaunch puts stability first. After the earlier data incident, we began treating “write → publish → keep it safe” as one chain: pages and documents are statically generated and served directly, media goes through a CDN, notes are kept in Markdown, and the key directories now hold a second, off-site copy. Even if something happens again, we can get back on our feet quickly.

We also tidied the entry points — intro, resources, team and docs now sit in one clear layer, instead of being scattered and hard to find.

## Polish as a Daily Habit

A community site becomes reliable through steady iteration, not a launch-day promise. So our rhythm is simple: small changes ship often, bigger additions land on a schedule, and navigation keeps adapting to real usage.

Rather than “stay tuned,” we’d rather hear “this could be better.” Tell us via the feedback corner or official groups — and we’ll fix the parts that get in your way.
