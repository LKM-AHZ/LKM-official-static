Phientist is entering a new phase of building. To make the organization clearer, divide responsibilities more effectively, and help more people who want to contribute find the right place, we have restructured the team and opened long-term recruitment.

The smallest unit in the new structure is the group. The structure consists of "three departments, one office, and one advisory group": General Affairs, Event Planning, IT Support, the News Office, and the Advisory Group. It also includes the Community Affairs Group and the Professional Committee.

## 1. The new structure

- **General Affairs Department**: the core of Phientist, coordinating all working and professional groups, setting the overall direction, and holding the highest decision-making and deployment authority.
- **Community Affairs Group**: a reserve and coordination force for General Affairs, responsible for managing community platforms, setting the tone, and keeping discussions healthy. Platforms include QQ groups, Tieba, Zhihu, and others; when a platform needs dedicated management, it can be split into a platform-specific group.
- **Event Planning Department**: plans, prepares, and runs events. Its core is the Standing Planning Group, which coordinates events across Phientist's platforms and QQ groups and guides the Professional Committee. In the future it will work with the Professional Committee and News Office on translations of frontier science content.
- **Professional Committee**: focused on professional expertise, science communication, and content output. It produces explainer videos, open classes, articles, and other formats that bring knowledge to a wider audience. As a field or workload grows, it can be divided into professional and working groups.
- **IT Support Department**: provides technical support and builds and maintains the website. When conditions allow, it will run external learning and exchange forums or develop Phientist's own software. If the team is short-handed, it will sit under the Advisory Group's technical support group.
- **News Office**: handles external communication, public relations, and promotion, including graphic design, posters, photography, and video editing. It will later take on offline development and science communication, and may expand into publishing.
- **Advisory Group**: evolved from the former advisory group, with internal and external advisors who provide technical support and consultation, legal advice, and suggestions on external exchange and learning. As it grows, it can be divided into specialist advisory, legal advisory, and other groups.

The division above reflects the current stage; specific responsibilities will be refined in practice. After new members join, General Affairs will authorize them and make an announcement, grant a purple title, add them to the Council, and provide corresponding rewards.

## 2. Transition arrangements

- The former General Affairs Group, Community Affairs Group, and Advisory Group will be contacted one by one to confirm whether they have the time and willingness to continue. Those who stay keep their positions; those who do not will be removed. The former General Affairs group will remain as a working group and no one will be removed from it; the new system will operate in the Council group.
- Existing members will discuss possible role changes, such as joining the Professional Committee or another working group.
- If the new structure has few people, units will be named as groups (for example, News Promotion Group or Technical Support Group). No separate working-group chats will be created for now; everyone will stay in the Council.
- Members with a role at Phientist should not normally hold a role in another organization at the same time. If they do, they should explain the situation. The Professional Committee is an exception because of its special nature.
- The rebuild does not have to be perfect from day one. If you have ideas, the courage to act and take responsibility, the determination to build Phientist, the desire to spread knowledge, and a love of science and engineering, the organization is willing to train you.
- Whether you have proven yourself through the group adoption program or a recruitment announcement, you are among the most important people in this rebuild. After the departure of founder Keqi, the organization has reached another rebuilding point; everyone who joins at this stage carries a special weight.

## 3. Who we are looking for

All applicants should:

- agree with Phientist's goals and our shared expectations for development;
- be determined to build Phientist;
- identify with the idea of "Phientist builds Phientist";
- be willing to contribute and work for the love of it.

## 4. Open roles

### Event Planning Department

We are looking for people who:

- have ideas and imagination, can judge how feasible an idea is, and think through how to make it happen;
- dare to act and take responsibility for the outcome, whether it goes well or badly.

> University students with more flexible time are preferred. Middle and high school students can start by planning a weekend activity independently.

**Assessment**: independently plan, prepare, and run one online event. This can connect with the group adoption program. The organization will provide help and examples, so it does not need to be intimidating. After passing, you will become a standing member of the Event Planning Department and share the current planning lead's work.

We encourage teams of two or three to try different directions. The organization will train new members until they can handle the whole process, from an idea to preparation, planning, and execution.

### Professional Committee

We are looking for people who:

- want to create science communication content and bring knowledge to the public and students, promoting the spread of science and technology. Specifically:
  - reduce the harm caused by misinformation and help break down knowledge barriers and information gaps;
  - make frontier and advanced topics accessible, so more people can discuss and exchange ideas;
  - give students learning opportunities and discussion beyond the classroom.
- can show relevant credentials, such as research experience, a degree in the field, or professional experience after graduation, to ensure the content is scientifically accurate and rigorous.

> This role also suits people who are busy but still want to contribute: as long as you can set aside some time for content output, it works. It also suits specialists who want to communicate their field.

We care about the quality of the Professional Committee's output, not age or years of experience. Whether you are a researcher or R&D professional on the front line, a student in the field or a competition participant, or simply someone with a strong interest in an area such as astronomy, there is a place for you:

- **Professional Group**: researchers and R&D professionals on the front line;
- **Semi-professional Group**: students in fields such as mathematics or physics, or people who have taken part in competitions;
- **Non-professional Group**: people who have not yet entered university but have an interest in a field and are willing to learn and take part in event planning.

Work from the Semi-professional and Non-professional Groups is reviewed and guided by the Professional Group before publication.

The simplest way to join is to stay active in the community, answer questions in your area, show your expertise, and take part in events planned or collected by the Event Planning Department. The organization will invite suitable people directly, and self-recommendations are welcome.

### IT Support Department

We are looking for people who:

- have strong technical skills and can understand and implement functional requirements and build what is needed;
- are loyal to Phientist and willing to help it grow, and take code security and confidentiality seriously, with a sense of responsibility for the team's work.

The department also connects with the group adoption program: once a founder opens an engineering discussion group, recruitment can begin. The organization currently plans to rebuild the website.

We will not treat technical ability or confidentiality lightly; your determination to contribute to Phientist's development matters just as much.

### News Office

We are looking for people with two or three of the following (not all are required):

- good skills in graphic design, video editing, or UI design — at least one — with an eye for aesthetics and inspiration;
- a basic understanding of multiple promotion channels and familiarity with social media such as Bilibili, Douyin, Tieba, Zhihu, and WeChat Official Accounts, enough for basic operations and promotion;
- sensitivity to news, attention to frontier science and technology, and the ability to share updates to the group promptly;
- good foreign-language skills — English, German, Russian, French, Spanish, or another language, with at least one at a strong level (not mandatory).

The organization will provide training and development. Promotion is very important to General Affairs. Before you have earned the organization's trust, News Office members will assist General Affairs; once the organization sees your commitment and your hopes for Phientist, it will trust you.

### Advisory Group

This recruitment is for core members of the Advisory Group. We are looking for people who:

- are familiar with the development of a field such as law, finance, economics, website or software launch regulations, academic exchange, or IT support;
- can point out directions Phientist can take now and actively offer ideas and suggestions.

The Advisory Group mainly covers areas Phientist is not yet familiar with but may encounter as it grows. We most need economic and legal advisors, as well as professional and technical advisors: we will launch fundraising to support normal operations and are preparing for the next community launch.

## 5. Other notes

- The general requirements are credibility and a clear determination to help build the organization. Ability can be developed; no experience is required if you are willing to be trained.
- After joining, General Affairs will authorize the role and announce new members, grant a purple title, add them to the Council, and provide corresponding rewards.
- This recruitment announcement is long-term and can be combined with the group adoption program.
- For details about the new structure, see the management handbook; if anything is still unclear, ask a member of General Affairs.
