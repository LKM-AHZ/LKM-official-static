The Phientist Science Outreach Initiative is currently underway and recruiting.

This project grew out of conversations with civil science outreach organizations. We talked about the state of science communication and what we might do together.

## The reality of science outreach

At present, the outlook for science outreach is not especially strong.

Manshi once estimated that people willing to watch math science videos make up only about 0.2% of Bilibili's active users.

People who enjoy science content are usually in their teens — an age full of curiosity and a desire to learn. Beyond that stage, far fewer people are willing to spend time exploring knowledge.

The harder the science content, the more sharply the audience shrinks. Without motivation and a desire for knowledge, the audience becomes narrower still.

## Why we are building this

That is why we decided to launch a science outreach plan: to connect people who want to make science content, people who want to watch it, and teams willing to support the work.

No single team can do this alone. We welcome civil science outreach teams to join us and work together for a thriving culture of science communication.

## Two content directions

The initiative currently has two directions:

1. **For young people and the general public**: make science easier to approach and more engaging, so more people are willing to encounter and understand it;
2. **For tech enthusiasts**: offer deeper content with more room for discussion, for audiences who already have a foundation.

Content will be designed differently for these two audiences.

## Build it with us

Our team is also working on the community platform, hoping to give the science outreach plan a larger and better direction.

Let us dispel ignorance and blind following, and rekindle the desire for knowledge together.
