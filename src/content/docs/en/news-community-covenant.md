The current goal of the Phientist July Team Council is to create a good environment for science and engineering discussion, increase the team's influence, and work toward becoming a nationally registered social organization.

Phientist is an organization focused on the development of science and engineering. Our purpose is to help more people keep their love for science and engineering, to show that this love has nothing to do with ability or age, and to let knowledge flow across the boundaries and barriers between knowledge communities. In the future, we will continue to promote science communication, provide a better platform for science education, and build a better academic environment and atmosphere.

As the organization grows, and to help Phientist go further, we propose the following covenant to all members.

## The covenant at a glance

- Embrace and practice "Phientist builds Phientist";
- Love for science and engineering is independent of ability and age;
- Reject posturing, feigned weakness, flattering the strong while belittling the weak, and cliquishness;
- Learn more, think more, and discuss more.

## 1. Embrace "Phientist builds Phientist"

Most people who come to Phientist love science and engineering. Even those who are new to it are willing to feel its appeal and keep a desire to learn.

The Phientist management team did not reach this point through one person's effort, nor was it built overnight. The General Affairs Department sincerely hopes that everyone can take part in building the organization within their capacity, so that it becomes more professional, more formal, larger, and able to go further.

We therefore invite everyone to embrace and practice the idea of "Phientist builds Phientist": together, let us make Phientist a better platform where more people can feel the beauty of science and engineering, and find room to learn, to love, and to contribute.

## 2. Love for science and engineering is independent of ability and age

Most of us meet online, but the person we are talking to may be a PhD researcher or a middle or high school student; they may have deep foundations or be just starting out.

Whatever their ability, age, or background, please stay fair, non-discriminatory, and free of comparison, and treat others as equals with respect.

## 3. Reject posturing, feigned weakness, flattering the strong while belittling the weak, and cliquishness

Phientist members range widely in age, from PhD researchers to middle and high school students. The fields we love and the paths by which we joined Phientist are just as varied.

The culture of the organization should not be shaped by posturing, flattering the strong while belittling the weak, pretending to be strong or weak, or small cliques. When discussing science, engineering, or a specific subject, focus on the content itself rather than abstract meme-speak. Do not make insulting remarks about other participants, and do not behave in ways that make others uncomfortable.

## 4. Learn more, think more, and discuss more

The organization currently operates mainly online, but people should not spend all their time idly chatting in group chats or staying online while neglecting their lives and responsibilities offline.

We encourage everyone to learn more, encounter new things, and think more about problems. Especially in content discussion groups, please discuss science and engineering topics as much as possible.

## A final note

This covenant is not a restriction, but an agreement we make together to maintain a healthy environment for discussion.

Thank you to every member who is willing to communicate seriously and respect others. Let us build a better Phientist together, and help more people feel the beauty of science and engineering here.

If you have suggestions or feedback about the culture of our groups, please contact the General Affairs Department in the community, or join the [Phientist QQ community](/en/about#communities).
