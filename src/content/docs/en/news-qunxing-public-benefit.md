This is the Starry Public Benefit Initiative that we are currently planning.

## Our idea

At the very beginning, we had an idea…

High school students help middle school students; college students help high school students; and people who have already entered research or society, in turn, help college students make good life plans.

## The information gap

On top of that, looking at the current situation, some very valuable resources rarely appear on the market, and how to obtain the resources you need is a question that really deserves thought. Whether you even know which channels can get you those resources is itself an information gap. Not everyone has a superpower that can erase the information gap.

As a result, a great many children study, enter competitions, and prepare for exams without resources or guidance. Some children are poor, or study what they love on their own without guidance or help.

For the wish and goal that knowledge can be shared, and in the hope of truly solving people's difficulties and meeting their needs…

## Why it is called Starry (Qunxing)

…we decided to make good use of the organization's greatest features — its huge age span and the many industries it covers — and launch a public benefit initiative called Starry (Qunxing).

The name Starry means that everyone can become the person they imagine in their goals, able to light up the confused night. When you need help, you draw light from others; when you become the person you imagined, you can light the way for people in the same confused situation.

## Join us

So we very much hope you will join in. This initiative welcomes people of all grade levels and all walks of life.

You can come and be a teacher — give a person a fish and you feed them for a day, but teach a person to fish and you feed them for a lifetime. Or you can write a document with your advice and put it in the community for everyone to read and look up. Or contribute more resources, upload more study materials to the community… and so on. Anything is welcome.

## What's next

To make the Starry Public Benefit Initiative a reality, we are now building the community, and the future [Learning Center](/en/docs/news-lkm-learning-center) will provide a platform to support the initiative.

But before that, we will also launch a corresponding public-benefit teaching plan, trying first to start some courses and to collect the advice everyone uploads and writes.

## About Star Orbit Tutoring Studio

In addition, our plans include the [Star Orbit Tutoring Studio](/en/docs/news-star-orbit-tutoring), which will provide personalized one-on-one tutoring to give targeted help to those in need, or those with the financial means.

## Our promise

And we promise here that the public-benefit teaching plan exists to spread knowledge and make more resources available to the public. We will not reduce the teaching quality just because the studio exists, or leave people with no choice but personalized services. All income and expenses and revenue splits in the studio will be made public. All income is meant to lay the groundwork for the organization's operation and future development, so there is no need to worry about the quality of the public-benefit plan.
