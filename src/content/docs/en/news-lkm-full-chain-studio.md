We handle the technical side — frontend, backend, testing, and operations — taking orders for all kinds of needs as well as outsourcing work.

Team member Tianyu has already reached agreements with two companies and secured the channels, so the studio is now formally in preparation.

The current tentative revenue split is 50/50. Once the studio is stable, with more channels and more people taking orders, profit will be more secure, and individuals will take 60% or 70%.

All expenses and income of the studio will be made public, and the organization's share will be invested in organization building.

We are also recruiting the studio lead, and a corresponding community group will open later. If you are interested, please contact the [Technology Committee](/en/team#tech) or [Qianxun](/en/team#general), a member of the General Affairs Department.
